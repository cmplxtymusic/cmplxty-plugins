How it works: Yoink searches YouTube captions for the exact words, cuts just that moment, and gives you an audio file.

Move the start and end flags on the waveform to trim. Press Play to preview. Drag the waveform (or the drag handle) into FL Studio, Ableton, or any folder. Only the trimmed excerpt is ever copied, never the whole video.
