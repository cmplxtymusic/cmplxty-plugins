# SMOTHER design

An original sleeve drum machine. The grid is a polymeter step sequencer; sleeves are time-varying processors drawn over spans of steps, with measured attack and release ramps. Kits are synthesized on the box (no third-party samples). The ALL row is a fixed 16-step 1/16 bar that colours the whole mix. Sleeve LOWPASS and HIGHPASS are 2-pole TPT SVF (Butterworth). The limiter ceiling is −1 dBFS. Inspired by the idea of painting processing over a beat, not a recreation of any existing product.
