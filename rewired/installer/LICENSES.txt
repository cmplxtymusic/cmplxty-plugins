What is installed
  VST3: <Common Files>\VST3\CMPLXTY Rewired.vst3
  CLAP: <Common Files>\CLAP\CMPLXTY Rewired.clap
  (all users: C:\Program Files\Common Files; current user: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common)
  Plug-in settings are stored in %APPDATA%\CMPLXTY\Rewired and are kept on update and uninstall.

Models and runtime installed with Rewired (Common Files\CMPLXTY\Rewired / /Library/Application Support/CMPLXTY/Rewired):
  spleeter4.onnx - Deezer Spleeter 4stems source separation model, (c) 2019 Deezer, MIT licence
    (https://github.com/deezer/spleeter). Converted from the official TF checkpoint to ONNX (fp16 weights) by
    tools/spleeter_to_onnx.py in the source.
