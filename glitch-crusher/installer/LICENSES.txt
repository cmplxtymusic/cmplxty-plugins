What is installed
  VST3: <Common Files>\VST3\CMPLXTY Glitch Crusher.vst3
  CLAP: <Common Files>\CLAP\CMPLXTY Glitch Crusher.clap
  (all users: C:\Program Files\Common Files; current user: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common)
  Plug-in settings are stored in %APPDATA%\CMPLXTY\Glitch Crusher and are kept on update and uninstall.
