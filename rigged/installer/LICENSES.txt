What is installed
  VST3: <Common Files>\VST3\CMPLXTY RIGGED.vst3
  CLAP: <Common Files>\CLAP\CMPLXTY RIGGED.clap
  (all users: C:\Program Files\Common Files; current user: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common)
  App:  C:\Program Files\CMPLXTY\RIGGED\CMPLXTY RIGGED.exe (current user: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\CMPLXTY\RIGGED)
  Plug-in settings and your own presets are stored in %APPDATA%\CMPLXTY\RIGGED and are kept on update
  (uninstall asks first).
